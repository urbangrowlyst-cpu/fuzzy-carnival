## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of independent local businesses in Mumbai and wider India: cafés, restaurants, salons, gyms, dental and medical clinics, and local retail shops. They are time-poor, practical, and usually not technical. They live on their phones and WhatsApp, know their regulars by name, and evaluate services by whether another local owner recommends them.

## Product Purpose

Urban Growlyst helps a local business get found on Google, earn more genuine 5-star reviews, and bring first-time visitors back with a digital loyalty card. Success means the owner books a free demo, then runs both tools without needing technical help.

## Positioning

Two tools in one loop. A QR code at the counter lets a happy customer post a natural, keyword-rich Google review in seconds, which brings in new customers. The same counter ritual gives that customer a digital stamp card, which brings them back. No app, no agency retainer, set up for the owner.

## Operating Context

The owner works at a counter or front desk, often on a phone. Customers scan a QR code at the counter, table, or bill folder. The owner manages the offer and sees customer and visit data in a simple dashboard. Sales conversations happen by phone, WhatsApp, and a booked demo. Pricing is quoted per branch in INR; multiple branches are separate sign-ups.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI-assisted Google review drafts: the customer picks what they liked, receives a natural draft, may edit it freely, and posts from their own Google account. Only genuine customers post.
- AI-written owner replies to Google reviews.
- Keyword-rich review content intended to help local search and Google Maps visibility.
- Digital loyalty stamp card, no app download, opened from a QR code.
- Owner dashboard: customer data, repeat visits, rewards redeemed.
- Per-branch QR code, offer, and dashboard.
- Pricing, preserved as-is: AI Review Page ₹4,999 one-time for the first branch; ₹2,000 one-time per extra branch. Loyalty program ₹3,499 for 6 months or ₹5,999 for 12 months. Prices exclusive of GST where applicable.
- Existing marketing claims are preserved as supplied by the owner, including "3x more reviews", "2x repeat visits", "5 min setup time", and "0 apps to download".
- Contact facts: +91 9152707095, urbangrowlyst@gmail.com, Mumbai, India; Instagram and Facebook profiles; WhatsApp chat.
- Pages: home, how it works, AI review tool, loyalty program, pricing, use cases, FAQ, book a demo, privacy, terms, 404.

## Product Principles

1. Show the loop, not the software: found on Google, trusted through reviews, back again through the card.
2. Speak like a neighbour, not an agency: plain words, real counter situations, no growth-hacking jargon.
3. Make the first step feel as small as a counter conversation: a QR scan, a stamp, a 20-minute call.
4. Put the price in the open; no "contact us for a quote" theatre.
5. Nothing to download, nothing to learn; the owner's phone is the product surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first, since most owners and customers arrive on a phone. Text must remain readable in bright shopfront light; controls need clear focus states; motion must respect reduced-motion preferences; all demonstrations need text alternatives.
